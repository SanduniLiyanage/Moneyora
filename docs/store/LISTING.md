## App name (30)

Moneyora: Budget & Receipts

## Short description (80)

A budget built from how you really spend. Scan receipts. Your data stays put.

## Full description

Moneyora keeps track of your money on your phone, and only on your phone.
It works offline, needs no account, and shows no ads.

**A plan from how you actually spend**
Once Moneyora has a month of your spending, it suggests a budget for each
category from your own history: steady bills at what they cost, everyday
spending from recent months, seasonal costs in the months they come, and
a buffer where spending is rising. Every figure says how it was reached
and how confident Moneyora is in it. Change any of them before you save.

Just starting out? Build a plan yourself: type a budget for the
categories you want and leave the rest.

**Know when you're close to the limit**
Follow your plan as you spend. Moneyora can tell you when a category
reaches 80% of its budget, and when you go over, and offers three ways to
deal with an overspend: take it from other categories, from one you
choose, or from next month's plan.

**Scan a receipt, skip the typing**
Photograph a receipt and Moneyora reads the shop, the date and each item
on your phone. The photo is never uploaded. It suggests a category for each
line, learns from your corrections, and keeps the photo encrypted beside
the expenses.

**See where the money went**
- Home is one screen: a ring of this month's spending, each category's
  icon and share around it, and big − and + to record an expense or income
- Swipe sideways for the month before or after, or pick any dates
- Every day's spending at a glance, grouped by day with its total
- Spending by category, income against expenses, trends over time, and a
  calendar of your spending days
- Filter by day, week, month, year or any dates, and by account

**Recording takes seconds**
Type the amount on the calculator keypad, then tap a category from a grid
of icons. That's it: the expense is recorded.

**Everything else you'd expect**
- Accounts in any currency, with transfers between them
- Repeating expenses and income, with reminders
- Custom categories and sub-categories
- Passcode and fingerprint or face unlock
- Encrypted backups you can restore on a new phone
- Export to a spreadsheet (CSV) or PDF

**Private by design**
Your data is stored encrypted on your phone. Moneyora has no server and no
account to sign in to. Nothing leaves your phone unless you back it up,
export it, or choose to ask the optional AI assistant a question, which
uses your own Google Gemini key and sends only category totals, never your
transactions.

## Data safety form (Play Console)

Answers that follow from `PRIVACY.md`:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does the app collect or share user data? | No data is collected by the developer. The optional assistant sends the user's question and category totals to Google's Gemini API, with the user's own key, when the user asks. | Is data encrypted in transit? | Yes (HTTPS to the Gemini API). |
| Can users request data deletion? | Yes: uninstalling, or Settings › Clear all data. |
